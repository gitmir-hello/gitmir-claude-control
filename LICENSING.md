license. Use it, read it, modify it, run it, share it.

The AGPL asks one thing in return: **if you distribute this software, or run a
modified version as a network service that other people use, you must make your
version's complete source code available to those users under the AGPL as well**
(sections 5, 6 and 13).

For most people this costs nothing:

- Running it on your own machine, for your own work — including work you are paid
  for — is free and always will be. Nothing to publish, nothing to buy.
- Using it inside a company, unmodified, without offering it to outside users — free.
- Forking it and publishing your fork under the AGPL — free, and welcome.

## 2. Commercial license (paid)

Buy a commercial license if the AGPL does not work for you. Typically that means:

- **Your organisation's policy forbids AGPL software.** Many legal departments ban
  AGPL outright, regardless of how it is used.
- **You want to embed this in a proprietary product** and ship it to customers
  without releasing your own source.
- **You want to offer it as a hosted or managed service** to third parties while
  keeping your modifications closed.
- **You need something the AGPL cannot give you** — a warranty, an indemnity, a
  written agreement your procurement team can sign, or support terms.

A commercial license removes the AGPL's copyleft and network-source obligations for
your use, on terms we agree in writing.

**Contact: [hello@gitmir.com](mailto:hello@gitmir.com)** — tell us how you intend to
use it and we will send terms and pricing.
